# PROJECT: ENGLISH ADVENTURE

---

## App Name
**English Adventure**

## Short Description (<= 80 characters)
A 3D English learning adventure for beginners with Buddy the little dragon!

## Full Description
Welcome to **English Adventure** — a stylized 3D fantasy cartoon adventure designed for children around age 10 who are starting English from scratch!

Explore **Alphabet Island** alongside **Buddy** the little dragon and **Professor Owl**, complete story quests, help island characters, and build core English skills naturally:

- **Structured Pre-A1 & Introductory A1 Content:** 26 letters (clearly separating letter names from letter sounds), 24 Phonics units, 115 Pre-A1 core words + 32 A1 sample words with unique illustrations, 48 everyday phrases, and 32 sentence patterns.
- **Balanced 4-Skill Practice:** Listening (with normal and slow playback), Speaking (listen and practice recording locally), Reading (word-picture matching and short passages), and Writing (spelling and sentence building).
- **8 Interactive Mini-Games & Story Quests:** Listen & Find, Word Builder, Word Matching, Sentence Builder, Sound Detective, Speaking Hero, Memory Quest, and Dialogue Adventure.
- **Kid-Safe & Offline-First:** Play downloaded learning content without an internet connection, with zero ads, zero public chat, and gentle feedback when making mistakes.

*(Transparency note: Voice playback uses device/browser Text-to-Speech where pre-recorded studio audio packs are not yet bundled; speaking practice records locally for self-review and does not currently perform live AI pronunciation grading.)*
